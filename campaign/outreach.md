# Champion outreach (post-activation, Day 14+)

## Who gets this
A person who (a) ran skillsdrift, (b) has a second same-domain colleague who ran it, and (c) touched the waitlist — an activated team per the strategy §3 definition. Contact via waitlist email, GitHub issue they filed, or LinkedIn if public.

## Email template — Subject: "You and a teammate both ran skillsdrift — 15 min to compare notes?"

Hi <Name>,

You and <colleague or "a teammate"> both ran skillsdrift recently — so your team has the exact problem it detects: agent skills drifting apart across repos.

I'm building the governed layer on top of this (skillsdrift is the free diagnostic; the pilot is the persistent version — identity, versions, owners, always-on drift monitoring for Claude Code / Codex teams). Full context: this is part of a GTM challenge for Atlan's Agent Registry, which is the product the pilot leads into.

Two asks, 15 minutes total:
1. What did your report show? (Which findings were real pain vs noise?)
2. What would make your team adopt a persistent version — what's missing in the report?

If useful, I'll set your team up in the pilot first and you keep the CLI free either way.

— Aryaman

(Only reference specific findings — "your report found 3 drifted skills" — if the person actually shared their report contents. We have no telemetry; never imply we saw data we don't have.)

## 15-min call agenda (when booked)
1. (2 min) Their skills setup: how many, where, who maintains
2. (5 min) Walk through THEIR report — confirm/refute each finding category
3. (5 min) What "governed" would need to mean for them (permissions? evals? traces?)
4. (3 min) Offer: pilot onboarding + feedback loop (they shape the product)

## Rules
- No sales pressure: they already activated; the call is research + concierge onboarding.
- Log every call in evidence appendix: date, team size, findings confirmed, objections, quotes (with permission).
- Max one follow-up if no reply after 5 days; then stop.
